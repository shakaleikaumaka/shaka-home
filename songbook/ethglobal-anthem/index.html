🌱 The story

Made with Harsha Karamchati for an ETHGlobal Istanbul hackathon project in November 2023 — the preview went out on 19 November (“Eth Global Anthem cooking…”) and the full anthem with its lyric cards on 26 November. The Playbook lines echo the ETHGlobal Playbook: get the smartest people into web3, make it accessible to the entire globe, don't compromise on quality, create more value than you capture. Words read from the video's own lyric cards and checked against the sung take (it sings “that's why”, the card says “it's why”). Chords were heard by the ʻohana's ear from the recording — a draft until the bard blesses it.
