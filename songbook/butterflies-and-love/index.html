🌱 The story

The Ethereum Singapore team recorded Shaka in Singapore — with Aya present in the studio. When he first played "Butterflies and Love" for her at the ArtScience Museum, it brought her to tears. He didn't know she was in the hardest year of her leadership when he wrote it — the universe gave him the song so he could sing it in defense of her leadership and wisdom: that Ethereum maintains its values and doubles down on what is true, in the garden from which C.R.O.P.S. grew. 🌱
