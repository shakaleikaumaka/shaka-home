Everyone Who Showed Up

🪷 a thank-you to the Devcon 8 Music Sanctuary · Mumbai, 3–6 Nov 2026

For the sound crew, the dancers, the players, the baby on the floor playing her first drums, and the quiet one who felt safe in the circle — the Sanctuary was all of you.

Verse 1 — the ones behind the desk

Before the first string rang you were taping down the lines, four tops aimed out from the circle, a monitor at every knee. You rode the faders for a tabla and a laptop and a child, and you made the shy ones loud enough to hear themselves be free.

Chorus

Thank you, everyone who showed up — the orchestra was you. No auditions and no gatekeepers, no wrong instrument, no queue. You sat on the floor in Mumbai and the floor became a hall, a sanctuary where the healing was the music, that was all.

Verse 2 — the ones who danced

You didn't come to perform, you came to move the room, barefoot on the rugs with the mandala on your back. Thirty-five of us took turns at the mics across four days, but the ones who danced were the orchestra, and we never lost the track.

Verse 3 — the baby on the floor

There was a baby on the floor and someone handed her the sticks, and she found the drum before she found the words. Her first beat landed late and the whole circle landed with it — that was the truest downbeat that any of us heard.

Chorus

(repeat)

Verse 4 — the one who sat in silence

And there was one who carries the weight of Ethereum's name who came in without a speech and didn't take a chair. Just sat in the circle, silent, with nothing to defend — and if even you could feel safe here, then everyone was safe in there.

Verse 5 — the singers, the saxes, the first-timers

Indian voices lifting ragas, saxophones answering back, DJs trading decks with live coders, a ukulele learning its three chords. Someone played for the first time ever and the room held them like water — every voice a node, every sound a contribution, and nobody kept score.

Bridge — the last evening

Friday the sixth, Dhanteras, the first lamp of Diwali — a brass lamp lit in the circle where the drums had been all week. Then confetti under the mandala and the whole of Devcon dancing, and the Sanctuary held the closing like it held the first one meek.

Last chorus

Thank you, everyone who showed up — the orchestra was you. From the sound crew to the dancers to the baby to the quiet one, too. We rest, we heal, we play again — the circle isn't done — see you on the floor in Lisbon. Every contribution is one. 🪷

📖 The story

Four days in one hall at the Jio World Centre, Mumbai: cane stools in a circle, drums on rugs, a mandala of light on the wall, about 35 artists and everyone who danced. On the last evening — Friday 6 Nov, Dhanteras, the first day of Diwali — the Sanctuary became the hall for Devcon 8's closing ceremony: a brass lamp lit, then confetti under the same mandala. Written at Shaka's ask: "thanking the Devcon 8 orchestra participants, from sound crew to all of you who danced and played, to the baby on the floor playing her first drums, to the president of Ethereum silently feeling safe in the circle — it was truly a sanctuary where everyone could relax and feel healed." Nobody is named on purpose: the circle keeps its own.
