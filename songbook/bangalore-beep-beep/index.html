🌱 The story

Sung live at Founders House during ETHIndia week, Bangalore, December 2023 — a song for the friends from Push Protocol, born from the beep-beep of the city's streets, where every horn is a message so nobody crashes. Words transcribed from the live take (sung twice — “I'll sing it from the top, one more time, why not?”); a few words were hard to hear under the room and are printed as best heard. Chords heard by the ʻohana's ear from the ukulele intro: a two-chord A – D loop — a draft until the bard blesses it.
