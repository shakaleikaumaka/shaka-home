Verse 1 — the door

We came home to Nimman with a suitcase and a song, the Shenzhen chapter closed, the poem still breathing on. Four seas on the door, and the old words are true: sìhǎi wéi jiā — wherever we are, we are home with you.

Chorus

(we are home, and the floor is being built) The sun came up over Nimman and the grid came alive, music is the instrument, and the instrument's in tune. Thirty-three who asked to play, and the garden holds them all — nobody in this family is out.

(the clock slept, the family didn't) The clock slept six days, and we said it out loud, a family that can say "we slept" will be awake on the floor. Own voices, own beats, any language is free — nobody is ever out, not even the clock.

📖 The story

四海 sìhǎi = "the four seas" — in old China the seas of the four directions were the edges of the known world, so the four seas came to mean the whole world. 四海为家 sìhǎi wéi jiā = make the four seas your home: at home anywhere; the whole world is home.
