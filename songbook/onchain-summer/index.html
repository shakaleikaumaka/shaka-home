Onchain Summer

four seasons of a nomad, building onchain ⛓️🚆

Summer on a train, October in Chiang Mai, November on the bus to Devcon — and forever for a friend and his fiddle. First published here on 24 November 2026.

🎼 Key: D (home) · starts on A🎸 Chords: A – D – G – D on every line · half a measure each🎙️ not recorded yet🤙 the bard's own song

🌱 The story

Shaka wrote it as one loop — A, D, G, D, round and round, like train wheels and a campfire. Each verse is a season onchain: the summer of building on Base, the October of pop-up cities and Zuzalu culture in Chiang Mai, the November road to Devcon with a bus full of friends, and the last verse — onchain forever — for Kuba, the fiddler, held in loving memory. 🎻
