The Steady Tide

(One Drop Is a Blessing, a Drop Every Month Is a Tide)

The morning the Blessing Pool learned to receive monthly — a kind banner on every door and five little tiers.

🎼 Key: C🎸 Chords: C – G – Am – F (the family progression)🥁 gentle sway, campfire-warm, half hymn half sea shanty🍴 CC0 — fork me like crazy

Seventy-five doors and every one was dark-tipped, nothing for sale, no ads, no toll on the sea, then Siri said it soft, like a shell to the ear: "ask like wikipedia — the kind ask is free."

Verse 2 · Am → F

So we stitched a little banner, gentle, polite, "this place is a public good, kept by gifts alone," and on every phone a big 🌺 aloha says hi — one tap and it's gone, and it leaves you alone.
