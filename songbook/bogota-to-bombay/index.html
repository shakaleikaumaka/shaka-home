📖 The story

A DIP is a Devcon Improvement Proposal — a public letter that asks: let us build a room where anyone can play. Mumbai's was DIP #8576 on the Culture Track (forum.devcon.org/t/devcon-8-mumbai/8576); the Devcon team gave the room, lent their photographers and held their own closing ceremony in it — we made them a thank-you book, opensourceorchestra.org/the-room/. Written at Shaka's ask: "directly thanking the Devcon team for granting every DIP, Bogotá to Bangkok to Buenos Aires to Bombay — we will be posting our Lisbon DIP ASAP, this time with even more support and love from the community in hosting this tradition." Devcon IX · Lisbon · 12–14 October 2027: the letter will be posted; a slot is a hope, never a promise. Join it at opensourceorchestra.org/join/.
