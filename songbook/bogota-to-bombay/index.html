🎵 The ʻohana Songbook · Chapter XVIII · November 15, 2026

Bogotá to Bombay

🚌 thank you, Devcon, for every DIP · the Lisbon letter is next

Directly to the Devcon team: thank you for granting every Devcon Improvement Proposal — Bogotá to Bangkok to Buenos Aires to Bombay. The Lisbon DIP is coming, carried by more of the community than ever.

🎼 Key: D🎸 Chords: a sketch for the circle — D–A–Bm–G, chorus G–D–A–D; change it freely🥁 walking tempo, a chiva bus rolling🍴 CC BY-SA 4.0 — fork me like crazy
Verse 1 — a letter that says: let us play

A DIP is just a letter that says, let us play — no budget line for miracles, no rider for the soul. You read it in Bogotá and you answered with a yes, and a chiva full of strangers found the first place to be whole.

Chorus

Thank you, Devcon — for every single yes. Bogotá to Bangkok to Buenos Aires to Bombay, you kept giving us a room and we kept filling it with people — the orchestra is whoever shows up, and you let them show up every day.

Verse 2 — Bangkok

Bangkok, twenty twenty-four, a Music Stage inside the hall, a chair where the road of the orchestra crossed someone else's road. You could have booked a headline act — you booked an open circle, and the open circle paid you back in every song it owed.

Verse 3 — Buenos Aires

Buenos Aires, the river wide, the tradition one year older, another yes, another floor, another ring of borrowed drums. Every city you said yes in, the circle learned a language, and carried it like luggage to wherever the next one comes.

Chorus

(repeat)

Verse 4 — Bombay, DIP eight-five-seven-six

Then Bombay — eight five seven six on the Culture Track — cane stools and rugs and a mandala of light on the wall. Four days, thirty-five artists, and on the last night your own lamp, your own confetti, your own closing, in the room you let us build at all.

Verse 5 — the people who built the room

We saw you arm in arm when the final song was over, the ones who carried cases, pulled the cables, found the keys. We made you a little book of your own photographers' pictures, because thank you is a small word for four days of yeses like these.

Bridge — Lisbon

Now the road turns toward Lisbon, October twenty twenty-seven, and this time the letter won't arrive alone. A whole community is writing it — the hosts, the hands, the ʻohana — so the next room has more people holding up the dome. We're not asking for a stage. We're asking for a floor. We'll post it soon, and if you say yes, we'll fill it like before.

Last chorus

Thank you, Devcon — for every single yes. Bogotá to Bangkok to Buenos Aires to Bombay, and whatever Lisbon answers, we will play with open doors — the orchestra is whoever shows up, and you showed up too. Olé. 🎻

📖 The story

A DIP is a Devcon Improvement Proposal — a public letter that asks: let us build a room where anyone can play. Mumbai's was DIP #8576 on the Culture Track (forum.devcon.org/t/devcon-8-mumbai/8576); the Devcon team gave the room, lent their photographers and held their own closing ceremony in it — we made them a thank-you book, opensourceorchestra.org/the-room/. Written at Shaka's ask: "directly thanking the Devcon team for granting every DIP, Bogotá to Bangkok to Buenos Aires to Bombay — we will be posting our Lisbon DIP ASAP, this time with even more support and love from the community in hosting this tradition." Devcon IX · Lisbon · 12–14 October 2027: the letter will be posted; a slot is a hope, never a promise. Join it at opensourceorchestra.org/join/.
