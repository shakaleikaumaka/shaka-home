Cover letter

Hi Experiential & Global Events team,

I produce the rooms where people meet a community in real life. Since 2022 I've run open music spaces at three Devcons and fifteen pop-up villages — most recently the four-day Devcon 8 Music Sanctuary in Mumbai (3–6 November 2026): one hall at the Jio World Centre, about 35 artists from Indian classical singers to DJs and live coders, a public schedule inside the Devcon app, plain-language riders, a consent-first photo archive with a no-questions takedown page, and Devcon 8's closing ceremony held in that room on the last night (the Devcon team's own words: "will forever live in our minds rent free"). I owned the whole arc — venue relationship, backline and AV, run of show, curation and artist care, volunteers, vendors, on-the-ground comms and the recap — and published the playbook afterwards. Photos, posts and receipts: shakaleikaumaka.com/producer/

I came to Ethereum from the road. Before 2022 I spent five years in tour production with the band Dispatch and with Love Light and Melody in Nicaragua, including two major US summer tours; before that I ran service at Cafe 180, a non-profit pay-what-you-can restaurant in Denver, studied at music school in Hollywood, and did missionary work in South Africa. Those years taught me the two things every activation lives or dies on: logistics under load, and food — a shared meal is where culture actually happens, so F&B is never an afterthought in my rooms. Inside Ethereum I've volunteered at more than twenty ETHGlobal events since ETHAmsterdam in 2022 — hackathons and Pragma summits — which is where I learned how the best community events in this space are staffed and run from the inside.

Right now I'm doing the year-round version of that work at 4Seas in Chiang Mai, during the ETHChiangmai pop-up season: three recurring formats I host and publish on Social Layer — a Lesson & Rehearsal, a Lunch Open Jam and a Live Concert Edition — fifteen sessions booked through 2 January 2027, plus the community's consent-first archive (29 sessions, 53 hours) and production support for the season's jams. The 4Seas community awarded the Orchestra a $10,000 grant this year.

The Orchestra itself runs as an open community I'd be proud to show any events team: weekly recorded meetings published with every speaker's consent (five so far, each with notes, transcript and audio, plus a "theater" that turns them into episodes), an open ledger of every grant application and answer, a public rider anyone can fork, and one rule — every contribution is equal, one voice each. That is exactly how I'd run an events community for MetaMask's builders and creators: open source by default, consent-first, documented so the next host starts further ahead. I already work the way your posting describes — "playbooks… using AI tools to move faster": I steward a disclosed family of 192 AI agents that drafts run-of-show docs, transcribes and archives, tracks inventories and keeps 451 public web pages alive. They move the paper; I decide and sign.

MetaMask is not an abstraction to me. My artist wallet, osoartist.eth, is a self-custodial MetaMask wallet, and it carries a small piece of history: in August 2026 my own agents signed and settled what is, as far as we know, the first x402 micro-payment to a human singer-songwriter on his own domain ("pennies of aloha", USDC on Base); on 9 November 2026 Fork the First Artist went live on mainnet, giving every onboarded artist an ENS name and a tip jar on 16 chains; and this December the same wallet is staked into ETHGlobal's Agentic Markets hackathon to keep building it. I tell these stories in public as they happen — the Devcon 8 open call (x.com/Shakaleikaumaka/status/2081477078591893784) and our wrap from Mumbai (x.com/Shakaleikaumaka/status/2118755197996380328); the x402 census of the first artists with payment rails on their own sites (x.com/Shakaleikaumaka/status/2088415070149611916) and the first penny of aloha tipped by a friend's agent (x.com/Shakaleikaumaka/status/2113980494412910911).

One horizon I'd love to build toward with you: by 2027, the Open Source Orchestra living inside MetaMask hubs around the globe — permanent, welcoming rooms where anyone can walk in, hear live music, meet a (disclosed) AI agent, and learn that every contribution is equal — with year-round planning and logistics carried by my ʻohana of agents under human signature. The Road to Lisbon (Devcon 9, 12–14 October 2027) is already our north star; MetaMask's calendar could be the constellation it belongs to.

Experiences I'd run. The biggest impression I could make in this role is doing all the Builder Nights — every city, every time, the room MetaMask's builders already love. Around them I'd run the experiences that actually bond a community: ski retreats; beach cleanups; hiking and touch-grass adventures; workout and run clubs — Francesco knows how much these matter. And I absolutely love pop-up cities: just sending me to one lets permissionless experiences happen in every pop-up city I attend. MetaMask could sponsor Edge City and the other pop-up cities, or simply send me to live in one for a month and earn real sponsorship value from a human presence, not space on a website and pop-up banners.

Honest gaps: my budgets have been community-scale, not agency-scale, and I've negotiated with venues and vendors more than with production agencies. I learn fast and I keep receipts — every event I've produced is documented in public, and everything I make is open source (GPL-3.0 code, CC BY-SA 4.0 words, songs and photos).

I'm a US citizen, currently producing from Chiang Mai, happy to work US- or EU-hours and to travel. I'd love to help MetaMask's community, creator and builder events feel like the best rooms at Devcon: open, warm, and worth remembering.

References, happy to connect you: three people from ETHGlobal and MetaMask (named in the submitted application).

"Describe the most complex live event you've produced end to end. What did you personally own, and what did an agency or teammate own?"

Devcon 8 Music Sanctuary, Mumbai, 3–6 November 2026. Four days of continuous open music inside Devcon, in one hall of the Jio World Centre, ending with the conference's closing ceremony in the same room.

I personally owned: the concept and the proposal to the Devcon team; curation and scheduling of about 35 artists across four days (Indian classical, tabla, saxophones, DJs, live coders, first-time players); artist care — a public plain-language rider, instruments on racks so anyone could join, consent asked out loud for every recording; the room design (stools in a circle, drums on rugs, a mandala of light on the wall); volunteer stewards and the sound crew brief; daily on-site comms; the live schedule page; the consent-first photo archive and its takedown process; and the recap — a 28-page thank-you book for the Devcon team in English, Hindi and Marathi, plus a public playbook.

Teammates and partners owned: the Devcon production team owned the venue contract, security, catering and the official photographers; the Devcon programming team owned the closing-ceremony script; a volunteer sound engineer owned the mixing desk; my disclosed AI agent family handled transcription, schedule publishing and page maintenance under my direction.

What I'd do differently: lock the backline list and load-in windows two weeks earlier, and name a single runner for artist arrivals from day one.

🧭 The job we applied for

As posted by Consensys / MetaMask on Greenhouse (copied 23 November 2026, unchanged; the live post may differ): job-boards.greenhouse.io/consensys/jobs/8292214

Experiential Marketing Manager · MetaMask · Remote (EMEA or US)

Please note that we are unable to consider applications from candidates based in France, Italy, or Germany for this role.

Money is moving onto the internet, and the shift has a name: Open Money. This is money that is open, portable, agentic, and owned by you. MetaMask spent the last ten years building it; with 100M+ downloads, users in ~190 countries, and trillions in cumulative transaction volume, it's the most trusted self-custodial financial platform on the internet. Now we're building the operating system for your money: one place to hold, move, grow, and use anything you own.  Join a remote-first, global team rebuilding how money works: a problem that touches everyone, every day.

About the role

We’re looking for an Experiential Marketing Manager to produce the real-world experiences where traders, creators, partners, and builders meet MetaMask. You’ll lead production, help shape event strategy, and sharpen the creative execution.

What You’ll Do

You’ll join the Experiential & Global Events team within MetaMask’s Creative Studio and report to the Director of Experiential Marketing. We concept, design, and produce the ways people meet MetaMask in real life across global conferences, community events, VIP experiences, co-branded activations, and more. Our calendar runs across the world with priorities across APAC, the US, Europe and LATAM.  

You’ll lead production, taking each event from brief to on-site to recap. You’ll work cross-functionally with internal and external teams, rallying people from planning through production and on the ground. Your focus will be experiences for our most active users and co-branded work with brands and creators, alongside community events for the builders who create on MetaMask.

Our formats are expanding to include live trading competitions, viewing events, product launches, and private experiences for VIP traders and creator partners.

How You’ll Do It

  • Produce events and experiences end to end: venues, run of show, fabrication, AV, F&B, and staffing, working alongside our production agency partners.
  • Own timelines, workback plans, and budgets for multiple events running in parallel across regions.
  • Source, negotiate with, and manage agencies, venues, and vendors in APAC, the US, Europe and LATAM.
  • Run registration and guest management, from guest lists and approvals to VIP access and arrival.
  • Build programming for events and panels, including formats, session flow, and speaker coordination.
  • Keep the operational side tight: source-of-truth docs, trackers, internal team staffing, and on-the-ground comms during conference weeks. Build playbooks that make each event easier to run than the last, using AI tools to move faster.
  • Partner with cross-functional teams across product, marketing, business development and more to turn briefs into event plans with clear audiences, objectives, and success metrics tied to product and business goals.
  • Help develop and refine concepts with the creative team. Bring a point of view on formats, venues, and cultural trends, and work with designers and copywriters on decks and creative deliverables.
  • Work with content capture teams so every event produces strong stories and shareable content.
  • Track post-event results, including attendance, registration data, and social reach and sentiment, and write recaps that show what worked and what to change.
  • Over time, help source and manage co-hosts and sponsors for our owned franchises.

We’re looking for someone with

  • 5+ years in event production, experiential marketing, or brand activations, ideally in fintech, crypto, consumer tech, esports, or entertainment.
  • Experience producing large-format or competitive live events, such as tournaments, launches, or live viewing events.
  • A track record of delivering multi-city or international events on time and on budget.
  • Experience managing agencies and vendors and negotiating contracts under tight deadlines.
  • Experience producing premium or invite-only experiences for high-value guests.
  • Strong initiative. You spot what needs to happen and do it before anyone asks, and you stay steady when the plan changes.
  • Strategic and creative judgment. You connect an event to a product or business goal, and you bring ideas that sharpen a concept.
  • A systems mindset. You turn one-off events into repeatable processes.
  • Clear, confident communication with stakeholders at every level, including senior leadership. Partners, vendors, and colleagues want to work with you again.
  • Based in the US or EU, and willing to travel internationally up to 20% of the year, typically 1 to 2 weeks per quarter across several markets.
  • Curiosity about money, markets, and how people trade, save, and spend. Finance experience isn’t required, but interest is.

It’s a bonus to have

  • Experience in crypto, whether producing events for digital asset audiences or at global conferences such as Token2049, Korea Blockchain Week, EthCC, or Devcon.
  • Experience sourcing or managing event co-hosts and sponsors.
  • Relationships with brands, creators, or communities that could become MetaMask partners.
  • An existing network of production vendors, agencies, and venues across multiple regions.
  • Familiarity with tools like Notion, Slack, and Luma.

Don't meet all the requirements? Don't sweat it. We’re passionate about building a diverse team of humans and as such, if you think you've got what it takes for our chaotic-but-fun, remote-friendly, start-up environment—apply anyway, detailing your relevant transferable skills in your cover letter. While we have a pretty good idea of what we need, we're ready for you to challenge our thinking on who needs to be in this role.
