0 · Before Ethereum — tours, kitchens, service

Five years of tour production before 2022: with the band Dispatch (including Madison Square Garden and Red Rocks) and with Love Light and Melody in Nicaragua, including two major US summer tours and a youth orchestra in Managua. Before that: service at Cafe 180, a non-profit pay-what-you-can restaurant in Denver; music school in Hollywood; missionary work in South Africa. Food and shared meals are where culture actually happens — F&B is never an afterthought in my rooms. Inside Ethereum I have volunteered at ETHGlobal hackathons across several years, which is where I learned how the best community events in this space are staffed from the inside.

1 · Devcon 8 Music Sanctuary — Mumbai, 3–6 November 2026

Four days of continuous open music in one hall of the Jio World Centre: about 35 artists (Indian classical singers, tabla, saxophones, DJs, live coders, first-time players), a public schedule inside the Devcon app, plain-language riders, instruments on racks so anyone could join, and a consent-first photo archive. On the last evening (Friday 6 Nov, the first day of Diwali) the Sanctuary became the hall for Devcon 8's closing ceremony: a brass lamp was lit, then confetti and dancing under the same mandala.

📸 Photos by the Devcon team's photographers, published with consent in the Mandala Room archive (35 photos, credits and the full set linked there).

3 · The Open Source Orchestra DAO — official, with meeting receipts

The Orchestra runs as an open community: weekly recorded meetings (published only with every speaker's consent — notes, transcript, audio), a public ledger of every grant application and answer, and one rule — every contribution is equal, one voice each. Funding asks are made for the whole circle and shared equally by default.

Honest status: a community ledger and a working meeting rhythm — not yet an on-chain DAO. A charter proposing on-chain governance is being drafted with the circle, in the open.

4 · x402 history on my self-custodial MetaMask wallet

I have used a self-custodial MetaMask wallet (0x2596…c934, ENS shaka.osoartist.eth) as my artist wallet across the whole road — tips, grants, staking for hackathons, and the first agent-to-human micro-payments I know of in music:

  • August 2026 — "pennies of aloha": an x402 v1 payment descriptor went live on my own domain (USDC on Base, EIP-3009 transferWithAuthorization, settled through a public facilitator). The first blessings were signed and settled by my own AI agents on 14 Aug 2026 — agents paying a human artist. Our own census says it plainly: first in time, not alone, and never "most".
  • 9 November 2026 — Fork the First Artist, mainnet: every onboarded artist gets an ENS name under osoartist.eth and a tip jar that pays 96 % to the artist on 16 chains; live switch is public at tip-config.json ("mainnetLive": true). Built at ETHGlobal Mumbai 2026; x402 loop on Hedera testnet; consent records on Arkiv.
  • November 2026: staked from the same wallet to join ETHGlobal's Agentic Markets online hackathon (3–10 Dec 2026), where the project continues with real artist onboarding and an audit pass.
  • ENS agent records: a one-page convention putting agent endpoints and prices in ENS text records, with the x402 pay lane as the agent:pay record (ETHGlobal Tokyo 2026).

On-chain: basescan.org/address/0x2596…c934. The address is already published on the Orchestra's open ledger as the gifts lane.
